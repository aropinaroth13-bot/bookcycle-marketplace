## Getting Started

### Creating an Account
1. Click **"Register"** in the top navigation
2. Fill in your details:
   - Username
   - Email address
   - Password
3. Click **"Sign Up"**
4. Check your email for a welcome message!

### Logging In
1. Click **"Login"** in the navigation
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**

## Buyer Guide 🛒

### Finding Books

**Browse All Books:**
- Navigate to **"Browse Books"**
- Use filters to narrow down:
  - Category (Fiction, Textbooks, etc.)
  - Condition (New, Like New, Good, etc.)
  - Price range

**Search:**
- Use the search bar at the top
- Type book title, author, or ISBN
- Get instant suggestions as you type!

### Adding to Cart
1. Click on a book to view details
2. Review:
   - Book condition
   - Price
   - Seller information
   - Book photos
3. Click **"Add to Cart"**

### Wishlist ❤️
Save books for later without buying:
1. Click the **heart icon** on any book
2. View your wishlist anytime from the navigation
3. Move items to cart when ready

### Checkout Process
1. Go to your **Cart**
2. Review items and total
3. Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**
4. Enter shipping address
5. Choose payment method:
   - Card (Stripe)
   - UPI/Card (Razorpay)
6. Complete payment
7. Receive order confirmation email!

### Reviews & Ratings ⭐
After receiving your book:
1. Go to **"My Orders"**
2. Click **"Write Review"** on your order
3. Rate the book (1-5 stars)
4. Share your experience
5. Help other buyers!

### Messaging Sellers 💬
Have questions about a book?
1. On the book detail page, click **"Message Seller"**
2. Type your question
3. Seller will respond in **Inbox**
4. Check **Inbox** for unread messages (badge shown)

## Seller Guide 💼

### Listing Your First Book

1. Click **"Sell a Book"** in navigation
2. Upload up to 5 photos of your book
3. Fill in book details:
   - **Title** (required)
   - **Author** (required)
   - **ISBN** (optional but helpful)
   - **Category** (Fiction, Textbooks, etc.)
   - **Condition:**
     - **New** - Unread, perfect condition
     - **Like New** - Minimal wear
     - **Very Good** - Minor wear
     - **Good** - Normal wear, fully readable
     - **Acceptable** - Heavy wear but readable
   - **Price** (in ₹)
   - **Description** - Be detailed!
4. Click **"List Book"**

### Bulk Upload 📤
Have many books to sell?

1. Go to **"Bulk Upload"** from your seller dashboard
2. Download the **sample CSV template**
3. Fill in your book details in Excel/Google Sheets
4. Save as CSV
5. Upload the file
6. Review any errors
7. Done! All books listed at once

**CSV Format:**
```
title,author,price,isbn,publisher,description,condition,category
"Book Title","Author Name",299,9780123456789,"Publisher","Description",good,fiction
```

### Managing Listings
View all your listings:
1. Go to **"My Listings"**
2. See all active and sold books
3. **Edit** listings anytime
4. **Delete** listings if needed

### Analytics Dashboard 📊
Track your sales performance:

**Access:** Click **"Analytics"** in navigation

**See:**
- Total sales count
- Total revenue earned
- Active listings
- Your seller rating
- **Charts:**
  - Sales over time (30 days)
  - Sales by category
- Top-selling books
- Recent orders

### Processing Orders
When a book sells:

1. You receive email notification
2. Order appears in **"My Sales"**
3. View buyer's shipping address
4. Ship the book
5. Update order status
6. Buyer receives book
7. Buyer can leave you a review!

### Responding to Messages
1. Check **Inbox** regularly
2. Badge shows unread count
3. Click conversation to reply
4. Be prompt and professional
5. Answer buyer questions
6. Build your reputation!

## Safety Tips 🔒

### For Buyers:
✅ **DO:**
- Read seller reviews before purchasing
- Check book condition carefully
- Ask questions via messaging
- Report suspicious listings

❌ **DON'T:**
- Share payment info outside the platform
- Send money directly to sellers
- Use external payment methods

### For Sellers:
✅ **DO:**
- Describe book condition honestly
- Upload clear photos
- Respond to messages promptly
- Ship books quickly

❌ **DON'T:**
- Mislead about book condition
- Ask buyers for external payments
- Share personal contact info publicly

### Trust & Safety
- All payments processed securely through Stripe/Razorpay
- Seller ratings help you choose reliable sellers
- Report any suspicious activity to support
- Verified purchase badges on reviews

## FAQ ❓

**Q: How do I pay for books?**  
A: We accept cards (Stripe) and UPI/cards (Razorpay). Payment is secure and encrypted.

**Q: When will I receive my book?**  
A: Delivery time depends on the seller's location. Most books arrive within 5-7 days.

**Q: Can I return a book?**  
A: Contact the seller through messaging to arrange returns. Our return policy allows returns within 7 days if the book doesn't match the description.

**Q: How much does BOOKCYCLE charge?**  
A: Listing books is FREE! We only charge a small commission on completed sales.

**Q: Can I cancel an order?**  
A: Orders can be cancelled before shipping. Go to "My Orders" and click "Cancel Order."

**Q: How do I contact a seller?**  
A: Click "Message Seller" on any book page to start a conversation.

**Q: What if a book is damaged during shipping?**  
A: Contact the seller immediately and report the issue. Take photos as evidence.

**Q: How do I become a verified seller?**  
A: Complete 5 successful sales and maintain a 4+ star rating to get verified status.

**Q: Can I sell textbooks?**  
A: Yes! Textbooks are very popular on BOOKCYCLE. Use the "Textbook" category.

**Q: How do I track my sales?**  
A: Use the Analytics Dashboard to see all your sales stats, charts, and performance.
